Frequently Asked Questions

A few quick answers before you get started.

How long does a website take?

Simple websites can be completed quickly, while projects with more pages, integrations or custom features may take longer. The timeline depends on the project scope.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. The websites are designed responsively so the layout adapts to phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Can you add WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp buttons and enquiry flows can be added so customers have a direct way to contact your business.

Can you help with SEO?

Yes. SEO-ready foundations such as semantic HTML, metadata, descriptive headings and other on-page basics can be included. Search rankings are not guaranteed.

Can I request changes?

Yes. The review stage is specifically for checking the website and refining it based on your feedback before launch.

What is included in the price?

It depends on the package and project scope. Pages, features, integrations and content requirements can affect the final price.
